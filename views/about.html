About Our Department & Alumni Portal

Welcome to the official Istanbul University Faculty of Economics, Department of Management Information Systems (Yönetim Bilişim Sistemleri - MIS) Alumni Portal.

Academic Mission: Fostering an enduring bridge between graduates, student cohorts, faculty researchers, and industry leaders to strengthen our collaborative alumni network and drive academic excellence.

Our Objectives

  • Empower Graduates: Provide a unified digital space to discover career opportunities, participate in mentorship programs, and join exclusive faculty reunions.
  • Mentorship Hub: Facilitate 1-on-1 guidance where seasoned alumni working in IT, FinTech, and Data Analytics guide current undergraduate students.
  • Institutional Analytics: Support accreditation bodies (YÖKAK, AACSB, ABET) by tracking graduate employment trajectories and curriculum alignment.

Academic Location

Campus: Beyazıt Main Campus, Fatih / Istanbul, Turkey
